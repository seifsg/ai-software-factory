# Owner-authorized local trials

Read only when this installation contains `references/local-app-diagnostics.md` or `references/local-small-change.md`. These host-local records grant no authority outside their stated scope.

Read existing records. The diagnostics filename may also cover efficiency. Verify owner authority, exact candidate root/revision, clean status, fresh independent assessment and rollback destination. Read the complete selected method/references. Record old/new identities and evidence limits.

| Situation | Route within existing authority |
|---|---|
| New project; no run/selection | Select the complete candidate explicitly: `--bundle <verified-candidate-root> --project <target>`. |
| Existing bounded fix | Use candidate Small Change as a separate trial; preserve original bundle selection and controller state. |
| Substantial adoption | Execute canonical Improve Factory compatibility handoff before changing selection. |

An applicable app-defaults trial takes precedence over a small-change-only trial within its scope. A verified bounded-fix trial may route before resolving the older bundle; retain that pin. Outside the trial's scope, follow normal resolution.

An old pin lacking Small Change/default guidance remains intact. Use a complete reviewed candidate only under actual trial/adoption authority; otherwise explain the gap and obtain the missing decision. No gate skipping, implicit global default, other-session upgrade or claim that a local draft merged. Launcher installation does not migrate a project or prove product improvement.
