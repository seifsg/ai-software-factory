# Finishing touches

Reuse the epic's one open follow-ups ticket. Do not create a second ticket or one per finding; keep its other deferred work intact. A small change without an epic uses its existing follow-up destination, creating one only when authorized.

Append each authorized parked finding once by source and finding identity:

| Finding | Source | Worth it | Why parked | Owner authority | Revisit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plain description | Report and finding link | notice + harm + cost = score/7, or unknown | One line | Owner instruction and source, scope | Condition |

Use `deep-review` for scoring and disposition. Safety, data, legal, required accessibility, unmet criteria and HIGH/CRITICAL findings stay in the active outcome. A low score cannot waive required proof or close a requested item.

When the owner starts Finishing touches, select the agreed entries as one batch with a checklist and definition of done. Keep unselected entries in Backlog. Use the complete batch before its final test pass; changed inputs or failures require fresh proof. State what was parked and why in plain words, with source/destination links separate. Only the owner's recorded decision can cancel or drop entries. Done still follows the merged PR.
