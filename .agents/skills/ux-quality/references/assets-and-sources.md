# Asset quality and current sources

Read when choosing/changing icons, graphics, images, motion, music, voice or effects. Reuse UX Quality's uncertain-choice comparison; no second vote.

Planning may research reference samples and rights. Testing our product's visuals, playback, interaction or performance waits until implementation/improvements finish; then inspect/audition in context before final automation.

## Choose and inspect

Use adequate coherent assets in the first usable iteration. Optional variants/refinement later. Required legibility, accessibility, rights and promised audio quality stay in scope. Inspect completed-iteration output before automation; no placeholder sold as finished work.

- **Icons:** reuse a coherent family/native set. Compare uncertain families: coverage, meaning at actual size, optical weight, filled/outline consistency, accessibility, themes and license. Label unfamiliar actions. No unrelated sets or random emoji controls.
- **Graphics:** inspect actual size/density, crop, transparency, color and light/dark backgrounds. Check compression, legibility, artifacts and style. Meet measured load/decode/memory budgets; maximum resolution is not the goal.
- **Audio:** audition in-product at representative volume/output, including repetition. Check clarity, noise, clipping/distortion, cut clicks, loop seams, onset latency, abrupt tails, loudness consistency, speech/cue masking and fatigue. Match voice/music/effects to intent. Codec/bitrate/loudness choices need target-platform evidence.
- Respect mute, volume, interruptions/audio focus. Where relevant: separate music/effects/dialogue controls, mono, captions or equivalent visual/haptic cues. Essential information survives silence; decorative sounds must not harass.
- Compare licensed/native/custom/generated assets on merit. Before adoption, verify source/version, usage/redistribution rights and attribution. Record IDs/paths, modifications and rights evidence. Competitor screenshots stay references. Purchases/new terms need authority.

Missing playback/render tools: name the required check; quality stays unverified. Metadata, marketing and previews cannot prove in-context quality.

## Research starting points

Checked 4 October 2026. Recheck applicable sources/versions for current choices; no fixed vendor shortlist.

- [Nielsen's heuristics](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/): understandable, consistent, controllable workflows; feedback/recovery.
- [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/): web accessibility; measured conformance, not checklist claims.
- [Microsoft icons](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/develop/ui/controls/icons): platform guidance; research the actual target's equivalent.
- [Xbox audio accessibility](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/105): volume controls, speech/cue clarity and applicable mono/pause behavior.

Sources inform choices. Actual comparison/inspection must support “best.”
