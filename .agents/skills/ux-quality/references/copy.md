# Copy

Read when writing or changing user-facing text: labels, buttons, empty states, errors, onboarding, emails, landing and sales pages, calls to action.
Keep the reader's language and the project's voice. Shortest clear wording. Keep quoted text, exact names and code unchanged.

## Sound human

- No em dashes. Use a period, comma, colon or parentheses.
- Cut generic openers, canned summaries, buzzwords, inflated adjectives, empty transitions.
- Specific nouns, active verbs. Natural rhythm. Vary sentence shape. No forced slang, no repeated templates.
- No stock antithesis ("not X, but Y"), aphorisms or slogan formulas. State the point.

## Make it scannable

- One reader, one need. Sales copy addresses one buyer or segment: their benefit first, then the feature.
- Specific headline. The body delivers its promise. One idea per section, short paragraphs, 1 to 3 key bullets.
- Key facts first. Familiar words. One term per thing. Descriptive link text.
- Cut repeated conclusions. Keep warnings and conditions.

## Earn the claim

- No invented facts, metrics, quotes, customers, testimonials, guarantees, urgency or proof.
- Verify names, numbers, units, dates, scope.
- Put proof and material qualifications beside the claim. One success proves no universal result.
- Missing proof: narrow or remove the claim, or mark the gap. Never invent evidence to sell.
- Label illustrative examples. Mark draft placeholders. Remove every placeholder before delivery.

## Make action clear

- Sales page, first viewport: who it is for, the outcome, a credible reason, the primary next step. Do not cram everything above the fold. No conversion promises without evidence.
- Answer the real objection before the call to action. Invent none.
- Call to action: clear verb plus honest outcome. Name the destination instead of "Learn more". State cost, commitment and conditions nearby.
- Button and link text match real behavior. One primary action. Keep necessary secondary and exit choices.
- Error: what happened, then the next useful action. No blame, no bare codes.
- Empty state: what belongs here, then the next action.
- Success messages are true. No hidden costs.

## Finish

1. Draft.
2. Check every claim and detail.
3. Tighten.
4. Read it naturally.
5. Inspect it in the real layout, mobile and desktop.

Stop when accurate, clear and complete.
