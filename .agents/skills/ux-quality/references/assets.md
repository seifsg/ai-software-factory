# Assets: icons, graphics, motion, audio

Read when choosing or changing icons, graphics, images, motion, music, voice or sound effects.
Planning may research reference samples and rights. Testing our own visuals and playback waits until implementation ends. Then inspect and audition in context.

## Rules

- Coherent assets in the first usable iteration. Optional variants later. Legibility, accessibility, rights and promised audio quality are never optional.
- Best fit beats price and file size. Silence may serve better. Deliberate stylization and simple sounds can be excellent. Inspect them.
- Compare licensed, native, custom and generated assets on merit.
- Uncertain family or source: compare three options. Do not replace adequate assets just to compare.
- Missing playback or render tool: name the check required. Quality stays unverified. Metadata, marketing and previews cannot prove in-context quality.

## Icons

- One coherent family or the native set.
- Compare uncertain families: coverage, meaning at actual size, optical weight, filled and outline consistency, accessibility, themes, license.
- Label unfamiliar actions. No unrelated sets. No random emoji as controls.

## Graphics

- Inspect at actual size and density, crop, transparency, color and the scoped theme's backgrounds.
- Check compression, legibility, artifacts, style.
- Stay inside measured load, decode and memory budgets. Maximum resolution is not the goal.

## Audio

- Audition in the product at representative volume and output, with repetition.
- Check clarity, noise, clipping, cut clicks, loop seams, onset latency, abrupt tails, loudness consistency, masking of speech and cues, fatigue.
- Match voice, music and effects to intent.
- Codec, bitrate and loudness choices need evidence from the target platform.
- Respect mute, volume, interruptions, audio focus.
- Where relevant: separate music, effects and dialogue controls, mono, captions or an equivalent visual or haptic cue.
- Essential information survives silence. Decorative sounds never harass.

## Rights

- Before adoption verify source and version, usage and redistribution rights, attribution.
- Record IDs or paths, modifications, rights evidence.
- Competitor screenshots are references only.
- Purchases and new license terms need the owner's authority.
