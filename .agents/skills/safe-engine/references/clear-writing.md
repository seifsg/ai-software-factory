# Clear writing

Use for new/revised human-facing copy: UI, pages, docs, reports, replies and skill instructions. Keep the reader's language and owner's voice. Shortest clear wording; every useful detail stays. Preserve quoted text, originals, deliberate reminders, code and exact names.

## Sound human

- No em dashes in authored prose. Use a period, comma, colon or parentheses. Cut generic openers, canned summaries, buzzwords, inflated adjectives and empty transitions.
- Use specific nouns and active verbs. Trim weak “there is/are” and needless “you can.” Keep necessary grammar. Natural contractions/rhythm; vary sentence/section shape. No forced slang, robotic fragments or repeated templates.
- Cut stock antithesis (“not X, but Y”), aphorisms (“the future of X is Y”) and slogan formulas. State the actual point. No padded punchlines or fake personality.

## Make scanning easy

- One reader, one need. In sales copy, address one buyer/segment. Lead with their benefit, then the feature/mechanism. Technical instructions lead with the task/result; no forced sales pitch.
- Specific headline; body delivers its promise. One idea per section, short paragraphs, 1–3 key bullets. Split real subtopics; keep required details/checklists intact. No section-padding to hit a count.
- Write for a busy skimmer: key facts first, familiar words, explained necessary jargon, consistent terms, descriptive links. Cut unrelated information and repeated conclusions; retain useful warnings/conditions.

## Earn the claim

- No invented facts, metrics, quotes, customers, testimonials, guarantees, urgency or proof. Verify names, numbers, units, dates and scope. Separate fact, inference and uncertainty. Label illustrative examples; mark draft placeholders and remove unresolved placeholders before delivery.
- Put specific proof and material qualifications beside the claim. Sources must support the exact claim; one success proves no universal result. Missing proof: narrow/remove the claim or mark the gap. Qualifiers cannot rescue unsupported facts. Never invent evidence to sell.

## Make action clear

- Sales pages: first viewport shows who, outcome, credible reason and primary next step. Inspect actual mobile/desktop layout; do not cram every detail above the fold or promise conversion gains without evidence.
- Address the relevant supported objection before the CTA; invent no objection or unnecessary friction. CTA = clear verb + honest outcome. Explain material cost, commitment or conditions nearby; no vague “learn more” when the destination can be named.
- Match button/link copy to real behavior. One clear primary action; retain necessary secondary/exit choices. Errors explain what happened and the next useful action. No blame, hidden costs or misleading success messages.

## One finishing pass

Draft → check claims/details → tighten → read naturally → inspect in context. Use the existing review; no new gate, scoring ritual or endless polishing. Stop when accurate, clear and complete; revisit changed facts or demonstrated problems. UI verification and automated checks keep the existing manual-first/tests-last order.
