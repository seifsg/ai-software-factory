## What remains before the wider factory is complete

- Host hooks and remote merge enforcement. Local controller gates do not intercept arbitrary actions or protect an unguarded push. Private-repository protection availability remains an external prerequisite.
- Separately protected acceptance and execution isolation. The local builder and records share an OS identity; declared inputs do not capture unknown mutable services automatically.
- The broader specialist library, authored with Seif: architecture, UX/product review, security, idea assessment, debugging, TDD, skill writing and managed worktrees. General review references and a manual demo worktree do not replace these modules.
- A second materially different project, wider browser/device coverage, and richer UI-quality evaluation. This fixture demonstrates specified defect diagnosis and repair; it does not establish autonomous aesthetic judgment.
- General adapters, multi-batch scheduling, deeper interrupted-process recovery and representative benchmark cases.
- Safe reuse of unchanged check results. This pilot conservatively reruns final checks even when improvement makes no code changes; reducing that repetition is a known efficiency gap.
- Broader modular packaging. Planning and execution have separate phases but still share Seif's original skill file. Release and observation remain outside this pilot.

These gaps do not prevent supervised local use of the demonstrated cycle. They do limit claims of universal compatibility, unattended operation and production enforcement.
